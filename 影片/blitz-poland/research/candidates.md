# Candidates (blitz-poland)

Constraint: no WebGL on this machine, 2D only.

1. **The last day of summer** (seed s1: Daedalia Planum lava flows; form: a letter being written; tone: suspense)
   Logline: On 31 August 1939 someone in Gdańsk writes a letter by lamplight; at 4:45 the guns at Westerplatte jolt the pen, and the ink line runs off the page and spreads across a relief map of Poland like a lava flow, each day a new tongue of ink, until the page is full.
   Key images: a fountain pen nib, the date line "31.VIII.1939", a pen stroke torn off mid-word, ink tongues flowing over a paper relief map (rivers Vistula, Bug as channels), the letter never finished.
   Worlds: one desk, one map. Form: one continuous shot, paper and ink. Wordless, ambience + music.

2. **Chalk myth** (seed s3: tin; form: a storyboard come alive; medium: ink wash; colour: chalkboard green and white; tone: mythic)
   Logline: A staff officer's chalkboard draws the legend everyone knows, Polish lancers charging German tanks at Krojanty; then a hand wipes it and redraws what happened: the uhlans charged infantry, armoured cars arrived, and a newsreel turned it into a myth.
   Key images: chalk horses at full gallop, chalk tanks, the sponge wiping half the board, the redrawn armoured cars, a chalk newsreel camera, the myth line struck through.
   Worlds: one chalkboard. Form: myth, then correction. On-screen chalk words + music.

3. **Gold between two jaws** (seed s2: painted churches of Moldavia; form: a video game level; engine: a rivalry that ends in a duet; tone: mysterious)
   Logline: As the front closes from the west on 1 September and from the east on 17 September, a column of trucks carries 75 tonnes of Polish gold south toward Romania; the film ends where the two invaders, rivals in name, hold a joint parade at Brest on 22 September.
   Key images: crates of gold bars, a road at night between two closing walls of colour, the bridge at the Romanian border, painted-wall fresco figures (Voronet blue), two armies marching past one tribune.
   Worlds: road, border, Brest. Form: a route that narrows like a game level. On-screen dates + music.

4. **The Polonaise goes quiet** (no seed; from sound)
   Logline: During the siege of Warsaw Polish Radio kept playing Chopin's Polonaise as a sign the city still stood; the film is the radio dial and the city's silhouette, and the music thins out shell by shell until the hiss.
   Key images: a radio dial glow, a Warsaw skyline in silhouette, the music bar by bar losing instruments, the hiss.
   Worlds: one room, one skyline. Form: sound-led.

5. **Thirty five days as a calendar** (no seed)
   Logline: A wall calendar from 1 September to 6 October 1939 tears off one page per day; each page carries what happened that day, the tears speeding up as the map behind shrinks.
   Key images: tearing calendar pages, a map behind, dates.
   Worlds: one wall. Form: countdown.
