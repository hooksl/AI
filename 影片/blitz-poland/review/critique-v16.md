# Critique: draft-v16 (960x540, 60 s)

Watched through the 1 s contact sheet, strips at 7, 26, 40, 43.5, 46.5 and 56 s, and review/qc-draft-v16/report.txt.
The film reads clearly. The crate of gold leaves Warsaw, runs the road south under the bomber, crosses at Śniatyń, and is loaded at Constanța. Then the east closes on the map, the two invaders parade at Brest, and the closing image is the ship at sea under the title. The first question (where does the gold go?) is answered by the last picture. Nothing is broken: no black frames, no freezes, no pops, no repeated pictures. Every caption is spelled correctly (ŚNIATYŃ, BRZEŚĆ, CONSTANȚA, 17.IX.1939) and reads as natural Chinese.

## Three biggest problems (worst first)

1. **13–21 s and 40–46 s · the map place names are too small to read, and at 43–46 s they disappear into the dark fill.**
   Viewer sees: WARSZAWA, LUBLIN, BRZEŚĆ, ŁUCK and ŚNIATYŃ engraved at about 5–6 px cap height at this size, which is about 11 px at 1080p and about 5 px on a phone. The Polish accents (Ś Ć Ł Ń) cannot be seen at that size, so the names read as "BRZESC / LUCK / SNIATYN". When the east fills dark brown (43–46 s), the dark labels BRZEŚĆ, ŁUCK and WARSZAWA sit on the dark fill and almost vanish (strip-40s: frames at 43.0, 44.0, 45.0 s).
   Smallest fix: make the map labels about 1.6x larger, and draw any label that lies on the dark fill in the paper cream colour (or with a 1–2 px cream halo). Leave the roundel and the type style as they are.

2. **8.0–10.5 s · the bomb bursts overlap the convoy, and the convoy drives on untouched.**
   Viewer sees: at 8.0–8.5 s the smoke column stands right on the bus roof, as if the bus were burning while it keeps driving. At 9.0 s a round burst is drawn across the third vehicle, half in front of it, and that vehicle rolls on unchanged (strip-7s, frames at 8.0, 8.5, 9.0 s). It reads as one thing passing through another, not as near misses.
   Smallest fix: move each burst into the road gaps between vehicles, or onto the near or far verge about 20–30 px away from any vehicle body, so that no burst overlaps a vehicle. The timing and the plane stay as they are.

3. **56–60 s · the last shot hardly moves, and its sound is the odd one out.**
   Viewer sees: a held seascape. The ship moves only a few pixels across 3 s (report: residual 0.91, "nothing moves"). The title writes on stroke by stroke from 57.0 s; the first stroke shows alone as a small mark above the sun at 57.0 s (strip-56s). The scene measures -20.3 LUFS, 6.4 LU below the film's median, with 2–5 kHz +4.1 dB over the median. That is the only harsh flag in the film, on its quietest scene.
   Smallest fix: let the ship drift right about 4–5 % of the frame width over the 4 s. Listen to 56–60 s for hiss or a bright edge, and if it is there, shelve 2–5 kHz down about 3 dB in that tail. The recording's quiet ending can stay as it is.
