# Critique: draft-v2 (60 s, 960x540, 24 fps)

## 1. 46.0–56.1 · Brest parade: you can't tell there are two invaders, and it runs long
What a viewer sees: in thumb 48 s and the sheet from 46 to 56, two identical plain red flags flank the tribune, and the two officers on it are identical too. "Two invaders side by side" reads as one army, most likely a Soviet parade. The caption is complete by about 48 s. After that the same composition holds for 8 s while tanks roll across (strip 55 s), so this is the stretch where a thumb scrolls away.
Smallest fix: make the two sides different. Put a white disc with a black cross on the left flag and keep the right one red with a small yellow star. Give the left figure grey uniforms and the right one khaki. Change the caption to "布列斯特 德苏联合阅兵". Cut the scene by about 3 s (cut to the sea at about 53.0 instead of 56.1) and give that time to the ending.

## 2. 56.1–60.0 · The ending forgets the gold, and its title phrase is wrong
What a viewer sees: in thumb 58 s, an empty sea and a sun, with a ship on the horizon about 70 px wide carrying a gold dot. Over this sits the title "闪电波兰". That phrase is coined. The standard Chinese term is "闪击波兰". It also names the blitz rather than the gold the film opened on (the crate at 0–4 s). No line tells the viewer that the gold made it out. A stray white dot sits just right of "兰" (about x 703, y 163) and looks like a speck. QC flags that nothing moves here (residual 0.55), and the scene is 5.0 LU quieter than the median.
Smallest fix: add a caption "22.IX.1939 · 黄金已在海上". Make the ship about twice the size and let it drift about 40 px over the 4 s. Delete the dot. Retitle the film "闪击波兰", or better, give it a gold title such as "黄金出海".

## 3. 40.0–46.0 · Map of the east closing: the labels drown and the country is cut by the frame
What a viewer sees: in thumb 44 s and strip 45 s, Poland goes dark and the black labels WARSZAWA, LUBLIN and BRZEŚĆ sit on dark hatching, where they can barely be read. This map is zoomed in further than the one at 13–21 s, so the north of Poland and "POLSKA" are cut off by the top edge of the frame, and the circle runs off the frame. "LUCK" is missing its Ł (ŁUCK), although ŚNIATYŃ and BRZEŚĆ carry their diacritics. The audio report flags this scene as 4.3 LU quieter and 2–5 kHz at +7.7 dB over the median. That is the harshest moment in the film.
Smallest fix: when the fill darkens under a label, switch the label to paper colour with a dark halo. Zoom the map out about 15% so the outline fits inside the circle. Spell it ŁUCK (also at 13–21 s). Bring the 2–5 kHz lead in this scene down about 6 dB.
