# Critique: draft-v6 (闪击波兰)

The story comes through when watched cold. The crate is stamped BANK POLSKI, the convoy heads south, the map shows the west closing while the route runs down to Śniatyn, the train crosses the border, the crate is craned onto a ship at Constanța, the east closes on 17.IX, the parade is at Brześć, and the ship is at sea under the title. Every Latin line reads correctly: WARSZAWA, ŚNIATYŃ, CONSTANȚA, BRZEŚĆ · 22.IX.1939. Every Chinese line reads as natural Chinese: 华沙, 八十吨黄金 向南, 西线, 越过边境, 康斯坦察, 东线, 德苏联合阅兵, 闪击波兰. I found no blank frames, black frames, freezes, duplicate frames, one-frame pops or repeated pictures. The picture is clean. The sound is the weak part.

Crane load at 32-40 s: there is no pass-through in the frames I checked (34.5, 35.2, 35.8 at 768 px; 32-36 in strips). The load hangs above the ship, follows it as it moves left, and comes down onto the deck between the funnel block and the bridge. The hook then lifts away empty. The ship starts to sail while the load is still coming down, but the crane follows it, so this reads as acceptable.

## 1. 40-46 s, plus the jumps between scenes: the sound loses its balance at the turn of the film
- **What a viewer sees and hears:** The east-front map (40-46 s) is the turn of the film, where the second jaw closes. By the numbers it is 3.7 LU quieter than the median, and its 2-5 kHz band is **+7.4 dB** over the median. So the big moment is played thin and harsh, after the 31-40 s port scene at -13.6 LUFS. The scene levels around it swing 4.5 LU: trucks at 5-12 s are -11.2 (+4.5), bridge at 21-31 s is -19.3 (-3.6), parade at 46-56 s is -12.1 (+3.6), ending at 56-60 s is -20.1 (-4.4) and also harsh (+3.2 dB). Overall loudness is fine (I -13.8 LUFS, peak -2.5 dBFS). The problem is the steps from scene to scene. Seen in sheet tiles 40-45, `strip-45s.jpg` and report.txt "sound per scene".
- **Smallest fix:** In 40-46 s, cut 2-5 kHz on whatever leads that scene by about 6 dB, or swap it for a lower sound, and raise the scene about 3 LU. Bring the trucks down about 3 LU and the parade about 2 LU. Raise the bridge about 2 LU. Raise the ending about 2 LU and cut its 2-5 kHz by about 3 dB. Then re-run sceneloud: every scene should be within ±3 LU of the median, with no harsh flag.

## 2. 4.0-4.83 s: BANK POLSKI is on screen for less than a second
- **What a viewer sees:** The stencil that tells us this crate is the national gold reserve starts at 4.0 s (sheet tile 4 shows only "B"). It is complete at about 4.3 s (`thumb-draft-v6-4.3s-768.jpg`), and the hard cut comes at 4.833 s. That gives about half a second to read the most important prop line. Most viewers will see a flicker of letters, not the words. The 0-4 s before it are spent on a hammer and a box lid.
- **Smallest fix:** Start the stencil 1.2 s earlier, at about 2.8 s, so it holds about 1.5 s fully formed before the cut. If the stencil timing has to stay, push the cut from 4.83 to about 5.8 s and take that second back from the parade (see 3).

## 3. 46-56 s parade runs long, and the title gets about 2 s at the end
- **What a viewer sees:** The parade has one picture with no changes from 48 to 56 s. The caption is fully on by 48 s, the flags, buildings and figures on the stand do not change, and only the tanks roll past (sheet tiles 48-56 look almost identical, `strip-56s.jpg`). Ten seconds is long for a point that is clear by 49 s. After it, the ending (56.08-60 s) is flagged "nothing moves", and 闪击波兰 is only up from about 57.7 s, so the title gets about 2 s before the film stops.
- **Smallest fix:** Cut the parade by about 2.5 s, to end at about 53.5 s. Give 1 s of that to the BANK POLSKI hold (2) and 1.5 s to the ending, so the sea shot is about 5.5 s and the title holds about 3.5 s. Let the ship drift a little more (about 2-3% of frame width over the shot) so the last shot doesn't read as frozen.
